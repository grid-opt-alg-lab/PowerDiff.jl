The IPP’s exposure

Mechanics

PPA settles at hub $H$. Spot revenue at gen node $i$.

$\text{Profit per MWh} \;=\; \lambda_i \,-\, \lambda_H$

Examples

  • MISO — Indiana Hub vs. western wind
  • PJM — AECO vs. PA gas
  • NEISO — Mass Hub vs. Maine wind

Question. Which transmission upgrades raise basis?

Pablo’s bilevel formulation

$$\begin{aligned} \min_{\overline{\boldsymbol{f}}\, \in\, \mathcal{U}} \quad & \boldsymbol{w}^\top \boldsymbol{\lambda}_\star\!(\overline{\boldsymbol{f}}) \\ \text{s.t.} \quad & \boldsymbol{\lambda}_\star\!(\overline{\boldsymbol{f}}) \in \mathsf{OPF}(\overline{\boldsymbol{f}}) \end{aligned}$$

$w_i = +1$ if $i \in H$,   $w_i = -1$ otherwise.

Outer: pick line capacities. Inner: full DC OPF.

Reading $\boldsymbol{w}^\top\boldsymbol{\lambda}$

$\lambda_i \;=\; \lambda_{\mathrm{en}} \;+\; \mathrm{cong}_i$

Energy component (system-wide) + congestion component (zero unless lines bind).

$\boldsymbol{w}^\top\boldsymbol{\lambda} \;=\; \lambda_H - \!\!\sum_{i \neq H}\! \lambda_i \;\equiv\; -\,\Pi_{\text{IPP}}$

$\min \boldsymbol{w}^\top\boldsymbol{\lambda}$ ⩵ max basis revenue. IPP is short the hub, long the rest.

Hub at slack: cong $\equiv 0$. IPP already wins. No upgrade.
Hub at load pocket: cong $> 0$. Upgrades relieve it.

Three obstacles

Implicit

$\boldsymbol{\lambda}_\star$ defined by KKT system. No closed form.

FD doesn’t scale

$m{+}1$ OPF solves per gradient. RTS-GMLC: 121 solves. Per iter.

Non-smooth

With linear cost ($c_q{=}0$), LMPs are piecewise-constant in $\overline{f}$. Need $c_q{>}0$ for smooth gradients.

PowerDiff: matrix-free implicit gradient

VJP

vjp(prob, :lmp, :fmax, w) → one transpose KKT solve. Cost: $\mathcal{O}(\text{nnz})$.

In-place

update_fmax! rewrites JuMP RHS. Preserves Cholesky factor. 

for k in 1:max_iters
    solve!(prob)                                  # forward OPF
    vjp!(g, prob, :lmp, :fmax, w; work=work)  # 1 transpose KKT solve
    e_star = argmin(g)                              # FW oracle
    update_fmax!(prob, fmax_k + Δ_step)            # in-place
end

Per iteration: 1 OPF + 1 transpose KKT solve. $m$-independent.

Sanity: 3-bus, full Jacobian

Cheap gen at bus 1, expensive at bus 2, load at bus 3. Line 1→3 binding.

Analytical $\partial\lambda/\partial\overline{f}$ vs FD: max error $< 10^{-8}$. FW converges in 1 iter.
